I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I completed my B.Sc. in June 2026. My research applies deep learning to medical diagnosis, with a focus on detecting neurological conditions such as stroke from facial images and routine clinical data. I have built clinical datasets from real hospital records, obtained ethics approval for patient data collection, and developed multimodal models validated on held-out patients. I am seeking a PhD position starting Fall 2027.

## Research Interests

- Multimodal Clinical AI (imaging + clinical data fusion)
- Explainable and Generalizable Healthcare AI

My thesis and publications are on the [Research](/research/) page.

## Selected Projects

**Multimodal Stroke Detection from Facial Asymmetry and Clinical Data**  
*PyTorch, EfficientNet, Siamese networks*  
Built a Siamese CNN that compares left and right facial regions to detect stroke-related asymmetry, fused with a clinical model trained on blood test data. Achieved a combined image AUC of 0.983 and 88.9% sensitivity on held-out external patients.

**Parkinson's Disease Detection from Gait, Speech and Handwriting**  
*Python, machine learning*  
Group project combining three patient data types with late fusion. I developed the gait-based classifier and the fusion stage.

**Federated Intrusion Detection for Healthcare IoT Networks**  
*Federated learning, CICIoMT2024 dataset*  
Trained intrusion-detection models across distributed clients without sharing raw network data, targeting medical IoT security.

**Deep Reinforcement Learning for Traffic Signal Control**  
*SUMO, deep RL*  
Independent project: trained RL agents to control signals on a simulated five-junction Dhaka arterial corridor.
