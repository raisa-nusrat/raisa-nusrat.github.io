**Course:** EEE 316 Power Electronics Laboratory, BUET (team project)  
**Tools:** Proteus, op-amps, LM317, breadboard hardware

## Overview

A bench power supply that delivers a stable, adjustable DC voltage for testing circuits.

## Design

- A Wien-bridge oscillator and comparator generate the switching signals.
- A full-bridge inverter and rectifier convert the power.
- An LM317 regulator produces the final adjustable DC output.
